1. Who we are

Digi Sanchaar, operated by registered business name, decides how your personal data is used on this Service. Contact: support email. This policy is written in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. What we collect

What you give us:

  • UPI ID (always needed, to send your cashback or commission).
  • Mobile number, name or email (Gmail) — only when an offer asks for them.
  • For Partners: UPI ID, mobile number, withdrawal requests and wallet history.

What we record automatically: your IP address, device type (mobile or web), the time of your click, the offer and referral ID you came from, a click ID, and the progress of your task. Google reCAPTCHA checks that you are a person and processes some device and browser signals for that purpose.

What we never collect: UPI PIN, OTP, card numbers, bank login details or passwords.

3. Why we use your data

  • To check eligibility and stop duplicate, automated or fraudulent claims.
  • To track your task with the Advertiser and confirm it.
  • To pay your cashback or commission and show you the status.
  • To give support, handle complaints and meet legal, tax and accounting duties.
  • To keep the Service secure.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

4. Your consent

By submitting a form on the Service you agree to this use of your data. You can withdraw consent for future use at any time by contacting us; we may still keep records that the law requires us to keep, and withdrawing may mean we can no longer pay a pending reward.

5. Who we share it with

  • Advertisers and tracking networks: only an anonymous click ID, not your name, UPI ID or mobile number.
  • Payment partners: licensed banks and payment service providers get your UPI ID, the amount and a reference so they can send your payout.
  • The Partner who referred you: sees only masked details (part of the number and UPI hidden), the offer, status and amounts.
  • Service providers: hosting, database and Google reCAPTCHA, who process data for us under their own security commitments.
  • Authorities: where required by law or to protect against fraud or abuse.

6. How we protect it

  • All pages and the API use HTTPS (TLS encryption) while data is in transit.
  • Data is stored with a reputable cloud database provider; access is restricted to authorised people and protected by passwords and server-side keys that are never exposed in the browser.
  • Mobile numbers and UPI IDs are masked wherever a Partner sees them.
  • We limit repeated requests to guard against abuse.

No system is perfectly secure. Please never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone, including people who say they are from us.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while it is needed to pay you, settle disputes, prevent fraud and meet legal, tax and accounting rules (financial records are kept for the period Indian law requires). After that we delete or anonymise it. When an offer is closed and deleted, its individual records are erased and only anonymous totals are kept.

8. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Our pages may use basic browser storage for features such as keeping you signed in on a page or remembering a sound setting, and Google reCAPTCHA may set its own cookies under Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it (subject to what we must keep by law), withdraw consent, or nominate someone to exercise your rights. Write to our Grievance Officer below. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.

10. Children

The Service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if you believe we have, please tell us and we will delete it.

11. Other websites

After you are redirected to an Advertiser, their privacy policy applies to what they collect. We are not responsible for it.

12. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with its date.
