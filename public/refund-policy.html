1. Overview

The Service is free: we never take any payment from Users or Partners, so there are no purchases to refund. This policy explains when and how cashback and commission are paid, what happens if a payout fails, and how to raise a problem.

2. When cashback becomes payable

Cashback becomes payable after the Advertiser (or its tracking network) confirms the reward-carrying task of the offer and it passes our checks. You can follow this on the status tracker: the task shows Successful when confirmed, and the payout shows Pending until it is sent, then Successful.

3. Payout timeline

  1. Task confirmed by the Advertiser. Timing depends on the Advertiser and can take a few hours to several days.
  2. Checks and payout. After confirmation we send the cashback within 24 to 72 hours.
  3. Credited to your UPI ID. In every case within 7 working days of confirmation, unless a bank, UPI or technical problem delays it.

Payouts are made by UPI to the exact UPI ID you entered, in Indian rupees.

4. Failed or pending transactions

A payout can fail because the UPI ID is wrong or inactive, the bank’s server is down, or a UPI limit is reached. In that case:

  • We retry where it makes sense.
  • If it keeps failing because of your UPI ID, send us a corrected UPI ID within 30 days and we will pay again after verifying it. We keep the amount reserved for you during this time.
  • If money left your account but did not reach you, it is normally returned automatically by your bank within the NPCI / bank timelines. Share the transaction reference with us and we will help trace it.

5. Wrong UPI ID entered

We can pay only to the UPI ID that was submitted. If you typed a UPI ID that is valid but belongs to someone else, we cannot reverse the payment ourselves, but we will help you raise a request with the bank.

6. Rejected or reversed cashback

No cashback is payable if the Advertiser rejects or reverses the task, or if we find duplicate, automated or fraudulent activity or a breach of our Terms. If a payout was made in error or is later found to be fraudulent, we may recover it or adjust it against future payouts.

7. Partner wallet withdrawals

  • Commission is added to the wallet when a referred user’s reward-carrying task is confirmed, and removed again if that task is reversed.
  • The minimum withdrawal is ₹50, with one request pending at a time. It is paid within 24 to 72 hours to the registered UPI ID.
  • If a withdrawal is rejected (for example for incorrect details or a policy breach), the amount goes back to the wallet. Wallet balances earn no interest and cannot be transferred.

8. Refunds

If any amount was ever taken from you through our channels by mistake, we will verify it and refund it to the original payment method within 7 working days.

9. How to raise an issue

Write to support email or call support phone (support hours) and include: the offer name, your click ID from the tracker, the date, and your UPI ID (you may hide some characters). We reply within 2 working days.

If you are not satisfied, escalate to our Grievance Officer, grievance officer, at grievance email. See Contact Us.
